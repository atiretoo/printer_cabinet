# Printer Cabinet Cut List & Bill of Materials (BOM)

This document provides exact cutting dimensions, material specifications, and hardware recommendations for building the dedicated 6' x 2' x 2' printer cabinet housing the **Prusa Core One+** and **Epson inkjet scanner**, engineered with **multi-stage vibration isolation**.

---

## Bill of Materials (BOM)

### 1. Sheet Goods & Lumber
*   **3/4" (19.05 mm) Baltic Birch Plywood**:
    *   Carcass side panels, horizontal structural shelves, plinth rails, pull-out tray bottom, and sealed door frame.
    *   *Includes two layers for the double-laminated 1.5" (38.1 mm) 3D printer shelf*.
    *   *Quantity*: Two full 4' x 8' sheets (or four 5' x 5' sheets).
*   **1/2" (12.70 mm) Baltic Birch Plywood**:
    *   Full-height inset back panel (critical for diagonal shear stiffness against 3D printer racking vibrations) and pull-out tray retention lips.
    *   *Quantity*: One 4' x 8' sheet (or two 5' x 5' sheets).
*   **1/8" (3.0 mm) Clear Acrylic / Polycarbonate**:
    *   Filament dry chamber viewing window.
    *   *Quantity*: One piece, approx. 20" x 12" (508 mm x 305 mm).
*   **1" (25.4 mm) Hardwood Dowels / Aluminum Tubes**:
    *   Longitudinal filament spool roller rods.
    *   *Quantity*: Two pieces @ 21.5" (546 mm).

### 2. Multi-Stage Vibration Isolation System
*   **Inertia Mass Ballast Slab**:
    *   1x **18" x 18" x 1.75" (450 mm x 450 mm x 45 mm) Concrete Patio Paver or Granite Surface Plate** (approx. 20–24 kg / 45–52 lbs).
    *   Doubles moving system mass under the printer chassis to absorb high-acceleration jerk forces.
*   **Decoupling Damping Mat**:
    *   1x **18" x 18" x 1/2" (450 mm x 450 mm x 12 mm)** high-density closed-cell EVA foam pad, Sorbothane sheet, or recycled high-density crumb rubber gym tile.
    *   Decouples the inertia paver from the wood shelf.
*   **Anti-Vibration Machine Leveling Mounts**:
    *   4x **Heavy-duty industrial rubber leveling feet** (approx. 2"–2.5" / 50–60 mm diameter vulcanized NBR/neoprene elastomer base, 3/8" or M10 threaded stud).
    *   Decouples the plinth and cabinet from floor joists / concrete slab.
*   **Low-Center-of-Gravity Acoustic Ballast**:
    *   4 to 8 reams of standard letter paper (approx. 20–40 kg / 45–90 lbs) stacked on the bottom cabinet floor to ballast the carcass and lower the natural resonant center of gravity.

### 3. Hardware & Mechanical Components
*   **Drawer Slides**: 1 pair of **22" (558.8 mm)** heavy-duty full-extension ball-bearing slides (e.g., Accuride 3832 or similar, 100+ lb rating) for the inkjet scanner shelf.
*   **PTFE Filament Pass-Through**:
    *   2x **PC4-M10** pneumatic push-fit bulkhead fittings.
    *   2m **4mm OD / 2mm ID** PTFE Bowden tubing.
*   **Airtight Gasket & Latches**:
    *   6 ft self-adhesive **silicone bulb or hollow D-profile weatherstripping gasket** (3/8" wide x 1/8"–3/16" thick).
    *   2x **Adjustable draw / compression latches** (or heavy-duty magnetic touch latches) to compress the drybox door gasket.
    *   1 pair **overlay cabinet hinges** (or concealed Euro hinges) for 3/4" door.
*   **Fasteners & Joinery**:
    *   Pocket hole screws (1-1/4" coarse thread) or Domino/dowel tenons for shelf joinery.
    *   1-1/4" trim screws for back panel and plinth attachment.
    *   High-strength PVA wood glue (Titebond II or III).
*   **Desiccant**: 500g – 1kg rechargeable color-indicating silica gel beads (orange-to-green).
