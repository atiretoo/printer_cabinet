# Printer Cabinet Cut List & Bill of Materials (BOM)

This document provides exact cutting dimensions, material specifications, and hardware recommendations for building the dedicated 6' x 2' x 2' printer cabinet housing the **Prusa Core One+** and **Epson inkjet scanner**, engineered with **dual-paver vibration isolation, acoustic wall tethering, 3D printer enclosure door with window exhaust, and shallow tool drawer**.

---

## Bill of Materials (BOM)

### 1. Sheet Goods & Lumber
*   **3/4" (19.05 mm) Baltic Birch Plywood**:
    *   Carcass side panels, horizontal structural shelves, plinth rails, pull-out scanner tray, shallow tool divider, and both door frames (drybox + 3D printer chamber).
    *   *Includes two layers for the double-laminated 1.5" (38.1 mm) 3D printer shelf*.
    *   *Quantity*: Three full 4' x 8' sheets (or five 5' x 5' sheets).
*   **1/2" (12.70 mm) Baltic Birch Plywood**:
    *   Full-height inset back panel (critical for diagonal shear stiffness against 3D printer racking vibrations), shallow tool drawer box (bottom, walls, false front), and scanner pull-out tray retention lips.
    *   *Quantity*: One full 4' x 8' sheet (or two 5' x 5' sheets).
*   **1/8" (3.0 mm) Clear Acrylic / Polycarbonate**:
    *   3D printer chamber door viewing window: 1 piece @ 20.37" x 25.59" (517.5 mm x 650.0 mm).
    *   Filament dry chamber viewing window: 1 piece @ 19.35" x 12.22" (491.5 mm x 310.4 mm).
*   **1" (25.4 mm) Hardwood Dowels / Aluminum Tubes**:
    *   Longitudinal filament spool roller rods.
    *   *Quantity*: Two pieces @ 21.5" (546 mm).

### 2. Multi-Stage Vibration Isolation & Ballast System
*   **Dual Inertia Mass Ballast Slabs**:
    *   **Bottom Base Ballast**: 1x **18" x 18" x 1.75" (450 mm x 450 mm x 45 mm) Concrete Patio Paver or Granite Slab** (~22 kg / 48 lbs) housed inside the base plinth beneath the paper shelf. Permanently anchors the bottom center of gravity directly to the floor.
    *   **Printer Platform Ballast**: 1x **18" x 18" x 1.75" (450 mm x 450 mm x 45 mm) Concrete Patio Paver or Granite Slab** (~22 kg / 48 lbs) resting directly beneath the Prusa Core One+.
*   **Decoupling Damping Mats**:
    *   1x **18" x 18" x 1/2" (450 mm x 450 mm x 12 mm)** high-density closed-cell EVA foam pad, Sorbothane sheet, or recycled high-density crumb rubber gym tile under the upper printer paver.
    *   1x thin rubber cushion pad under the bottom ballast paver.
*   **Acoustic Wall Tether**:
    *   1x **Heavy-duty steel angle bracket with neoprene rubber isolation grommet/bushing** mounted at the upper rear of the cabinet. Secures to a wall stud with a lag screw through the rubber grommet to eliminate tall-cabinet sway while acoustically isolating motor vibrations from wall framing.
*   **Anti-Vibration Machine Leveling Mounts**:
    *   4x **Heavy-duty industrial rubber leveling feet** (approx. 2"–2.5" / 50–60 mm diameter vulcanized NBR/neoprene elastomer base, 3/8" or M10 threaded stud) under the plinth corners.

### 3. Ventilation & Enclosure Hardware
*   **Exhaust Fan System**:
    *   1x **120 mm AC/DC Enclosure Exhaust Fan** (e.g. AC Infinity AXIAL or Noctua NF-A12x25).
    *   1x **4" (100 mm) Round Duct Flange Collar Adapter** mounted to the rear panel exhaust port.
    *   1x **4" flexible aluminum/silicone duct hose** to vent air directly to an external room window or window discharge panel.
*   **Drawer Slides**:
    *   **Scanner Shelf**: 1 pair of **22" (558.8 mm)** heavy-duty full-extension ball-bearing slides (100+ lb rating).
    *   **Shallow Tool Drawer**: 1 pair of **22" (558.8 mm)** full-extension ball-bearing slides (50–100 lb rating).
*   **Doors & Latches**:
    *   1 pair overlay cabinet hinges for 3D printer chamber door + modern vertical pull handle + magnetic touch catches.
    *   1 pair overlay cabinet hinges for upper drybox door + compression draw latches + silicone bulb gasket.
*   **PTFE Filament Pass-Through**:
    *   2x **PC4-M10** pneumatic push-fit bulkhead fittings.
    *   2m **4mm OD / 2mm ID** PTFE Bowden tubing.
*   **Fasteners & Joinery**:
    *   Pocket hole screws (1-1/4" coarse thread) or Domino/dowel tenons.
    *   High-strength PVA wood glue (Titebond II or III).
*   **Desiccant**: 500g – 1kg rechargeable color-indicating silica gel beads.
